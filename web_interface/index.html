Rules

  • Match the suit or value of the top discard card
  • 8s are wild (can be played on anything)
  • 2s make the next player draw 2 cards
  • Jacks skip the next player's turn
  • Queen of Spades makes next player draw 5 cards
  • Empty your hand to reduce your wild number
  • First to play all cards when wild=1 (Ace) wins!
